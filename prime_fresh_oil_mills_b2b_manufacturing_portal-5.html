4-Stage Quality Protocol

Engineered for Zero Adulteration & Export-Grade Purity

Institutional buyers require verifiable data. Here is how Prime Fresh Oil Mills guarantees consistent pungency, viscosity, and nutritional integrity across every metric ton.

STAGE 01

Optical Sortex & Cleaning

Raw mustard and sesame seeds undergo multi-stage de-stoning, magnetic separation, and CCD optical sortex cleaning to achieve 99.8% physical purity before crushing.

STAGE 02

Low-Temp Kachi Ghani

Controlled slow-speed extraction below 45°C preserves natural Allyl Isothiocyanate (pungency), Vitamin E tocopherols, and golden-yellow carotenoids without chemical solvents.

STAGE 03

Micron Cloth Filtration

Double-stage filter press & 5-micron polishing bags remove suspended seed meal and moisture (<0.08%), ensuring crystal clarity and a 12-month rancidity-free shelf life.

STAGE 04

GLC & Argemone Testing

In-house lab verifies FFA, Iodine value, Bellier Turbidity Temperature, and mandatory Negative Argemone test before sealing every drum, tin, or tanker with tamper-evident locks.
